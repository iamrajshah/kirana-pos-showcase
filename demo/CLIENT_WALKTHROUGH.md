## A useful sales conversation

Ask the shop owner which task causes the most difficulty: billing, unpaid
balances, stock, supplier payments or orders from regular customers. Demonstrate
that workflow first, then show how the records stay connected. Offer a guided
private seven-day trial.

Suggested introduction:

> Kirana POS brings billing, customer khata, stock and supplier payments into one
> application. Your customers can also order through your shop's own online
> link, with orders connected to fulfilment and payment. Let us demonstrate
> your main daily workflow, then start a private seven-day trial.

## Scope of the demonstration

- Estimated profit uses recorded product cost. It is internal guidance and is
  never printed on the customer's receipt.
- Cash/UPI/card are recorded payment modes. The walkthrough does not claim an
  automated payment-gateway settlement integration.
- WhatsApp list import uses text pasted by the shop team. Message sharing opens
  WhatsApp for the user to send.
- GST/CA reports prepare records and exports. They do not file returns or create
  statutory e-invoices/e-way bills.
- The present web workflows require connectivity. Offline billing and full
  bank reconciliation remain later work.
- Android packaging exists. Public Play Store/App Store availability is not
  claimed by this video.
- Plans and permissions control availability. Confirm the current configured
  catalogue when discussing a paid package.

The footage is a recorded demonstration of the verified workflows, not a claim
that every possible device, printer, tax scenario or operating condition has
been certified. New release checks are documented separately from this video.
