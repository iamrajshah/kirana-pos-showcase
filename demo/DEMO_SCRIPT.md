# Kirana POS — 60-second demo script

## Opening (0–5 seconds)

“Most POS systems only record bills. Kirana POS also tells the shopkeeper what needs attention next.”

Start on the owner dashboard.

## Owner dashboard (5–18 seconds)

“The owner immediately sees money to collect, products running low, and bills that were held at the counter. Each card leads directly to the action—not another report.”

Show **Money to collect**, **Stock running low**, and **Held bills**.

## Fast walk-in billing (18–35 seconds)

“For the common one-or-two-item customer, we do not force customer creation. Search or scan the product, change quantity if required, and collect by cash, UPI, or card.”

Open **Billing**, search for **India Gate**, and add the product. Point to the walk-in customer banner and payment choices. Do not complete a real payment during the recording.

## Product and barcode workflow (35–48 seconds)

“Loose and store-packed products can have their own reusable barcode. The shopkeeper can configure the label, include price and pack details, and print the required quantity.”

Open **Products**, select a product, and choose **Generate or print barcode label**.

## Mobile proof (48–56 seconds)

“The same workflow adapts to a phone at the counter and a larger owner screen in the office.”

Resize to mobile or show `07-owner-dashboard-mobile.png`.

## Closing (56–60 seconds)

“Kirana POS: faster billing, clearer decisions, and less daily bookkeeping—built for the dukandaar.”

## Recording tips

- Record at 1280×720 or 1920×1080.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Use the seeded owner account and demo data.
- Pause briefly after each navigation so the audience can read the screen.
- Keep the final recording between 45 and 60 seconds.
- Avoid showing passwords, deployment consoles, browser bookmarks, or developer tools.
